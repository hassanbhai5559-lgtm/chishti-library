💻 My Journey

Mera naam Ali Hassan hai.

Ek din mere Dada Abu ne mujh se kaha:

"Koi cheez banao."

Us waqt mujhe web development ke baare mein kuch bhi nahi pata tha, lekin mere andar kuch naya seekhne ka jazba tha.

Maine unka mobile liya aur internet par search karna shuru kiya. YouTube par free website banana seekha aur lagbhag 30 minutes tak videos dekhta raha.

Uske baad maine apne Chachu ka computer on kiya aur ChatGPT ki madad se coding shuru ki. Lagbhag 2–3 ghante baad maine apne Dada Abu se kaha:

"Website ban gayi hai."

Jab website khuli to usme sirf 3 boxes the. Do boxes par Unknown likha tha aur teesre box par Kalam Saim Chishti.

Shayad woh website bohot simple thi, lekin mere liye wahi meri pehli kamyabi thi. Usi din se mera web development ka safar shuru hua.

Roz kuch naya seekhta gaya, naye projects banata gaya, aur dheere dheere web development meri zindagi ka hissa ban gaya.

Aaj usi safar ka nateeja Chishti Library aur Chishti AI hai.

Mera maqsad sirf websites banana nahi, balke Hazrat Allama Saim Chishti (رحمہ اللہ علیہ) ke ilmi virsay ko digital soorat mein mehfooz karna aur duniya bhar ke logon tak pohanchana hai.

"Har badi kahani ek chhote se qadam se shuru hoti hai. Meri kahani bhi sirf 3 boxes wali ek chhoti si website se shuru hui."
